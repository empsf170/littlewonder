Our Story & Mission

Creating Wonder for Every Child

LittleWonder is India's leading discovery platform connecting curious kids and loving parents with unforgettable workshops, camps, and family experiences.

Why We Started

Every weekend should be an adventure.

In a digital-first world, finding quality, screen-free activities that nurture imagination and bond families can be overwhelming. LittleWonder was born out of a simple vision: to curate safe, enriching, and joyful experiences for children of all age groups.

From interactive theater and sensory art classes to outdoor nature trails and STEM workshops, we partner with verified creators and venues to ensure every event meets strict quality and safety standards.
