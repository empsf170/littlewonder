Magical Storytelling Circle

Saturday, Sept 6, 2026 11:00 AM – 12:30 PM LittleWonder Studio, Adyar Ages 4–6 Years

About This Experience

Step into a realm of wonder and imagination! The Magical Storytelling Circle is a captivating 90-minute interactive session designed to ignite children's love for reading, theater, and creative expression.

Guided by master storyteller Priya Ramesh, kids will journey through enchanted forests, meet friendly dragon puppets, and participate in lively sing-alongs and hands-on prop making.

What's Included:

Live Interactive Storytelling
Puppet Performance & Songs
Take-home Story Craft Kit
Healthy Juice & Snack Box
